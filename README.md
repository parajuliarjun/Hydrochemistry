### Hydrochemistry: Chemical De-acidification of Corrosive Water

This repository contains my laboratory report for the module **MHSE08 Hydrochemistry** at TU Dresden (Institute of Water Chemistry).

---

### Experiment Overview
* **Objective:** Evaluate the efficiency of four commercial filter materials (*HYDROLIT-Ca, Hydro-Carbonate, AKDOLIT-GRAN, MAGNO-DOL*) for the chemical de-acidification of corrosive model water to establish calco-carbonic equilibrium.
* **Parameters Analyzed:** pH, electrical conductivity (K<sub>25°C</sub>), acid neutralizing capacity (ANC<sub>4.3</sub> & ANC<sub>8.2</sub>), base neutralizing capacity (BNC<sub>8.2</sub>), Calcium (Ca<sup>2+</sup>), Magnesium (Mg<sup>2+</sup>), and Langelier Saturation Index (SI).

---

### Key Findings
* **Optimal Material:** **HYDROLIT-Ca** (CaCO<sub>3</sub> with 10% MgCO<sub>3</sub>) successfully established calco-carbonic equilibrium (SI = 0.21) and met the chemical requirements of the German Drinking Water Ordinance.
* **Filter Comparisons:** Pure calcium carbonate showed slow reaction kinetics, whereas semi-burnt dolomite materials (Akdolit-Gran and Magno-Dol) were highly reactive, overshooting into scaling conditions (SI > 0) with high pH values exceeding drinking water limits.
